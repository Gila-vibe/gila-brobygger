# Project: Gila Brobygger

## Purpose
Present Gila Brobygger's support, guidance and communication services in a clear, calm and trustworthy way, and make it easy for visitors to make contact.

## Organisation scope
Gila Brobygger is treated as the complete organisation and its complete website. The family consultant service is one part of the organisation's offering, not the entirety of the project. All existing and future pages, services, activities and content belonging to Gila Brobygger must be covered consistently.

Services identified so far (from owner-supplied inputs and existing website content; not independently verified):
- Family support and guidance (familiekonsult).
- Interpretation services (face-to-face, telephone, video and written translation).
- Social mentor / mentorforløb.

## Confirmed project inputs
- Domain supplied by owner: `gila-brobygger.dk`
- Contact email supplied in the original page: `kontakt@gila-brobygger.dk`
- Initial service content supplied by owner: family support, guidance about children's wellbeing and development, contact with schools/institutions/authorities, social benefits and family rights, conflict handling, communication, practical everyday support.
- Existing visual direction supplied by owner: green palette, light background, clean cards and responsive layout.

These inputs are not independent verification of mailbox existence, legal identity, professional credentials or the full range of services.

## Audience
Families and caregivers seeking information about support and guidance. Write in accessible, calm Danish.

## Primary goals
1. Explain the service clearly.
2. Build trust without unsupported promises.
3. Make the email contact action obvious and functional.
4. Work well on mobile, tablet and desktop.
5. Be accessible, fast and easy to maintain.

## Out of scope unless approved
- Online booking, user accounts or case management.
- Collection of sensitive family or health information.
- Payment processing.
- Chatbots, analytics, advertising pixels or newsletter signup.
- Legal advice or claims about statutory entitlements.

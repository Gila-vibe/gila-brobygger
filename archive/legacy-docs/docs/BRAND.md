# Brand and content guidelines

## Visual direction
- Primary green: `#246b45`
- Deep green: `#17452f`
- Soft green background: `#f5f9f5`
- Main text: `#23332a`
- Muted text: `#647269`
- White surfaces with subtle green-grey borders.

## Voice
- Danish, clear, warm, respectful and professional.
- Use short sentences and concrete wording.
- Speak to families without blame, stigma or assumptions.
- Explain professional or administrative terms in everyday language.
- Avoid fear-based marketing, pressure and exaggerated promises.

## Content guardrails
- Do not imply that every family receives the same intervention.
- Do not guarantee outcomes.
- Do not present general information as individual legal, medical or psychological advice.
- Do not publish case stories, names, photographs or testimonials without documented permission and privacy review.

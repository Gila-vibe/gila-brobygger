## 2. Purpose and values

Gila Brobygger works as a bridge between people, languages and Danish systems. The purpose that
emerges consistently from the existing material is:

> To make communication possible and understandable – in meetings, documents and everyday life –
> with respect for the person's background, situation and dignity.

Values used on the website and in the work:

1. **Respect** – meet people without prejudice; adapt to the person and situation.
2. **Clarity** – precise, plain communication; no jargon where it can be avoided.
3. **Trust and confidentiality** – handle sensitive situations discreetly.
4. **Practical help** – solutions that work in everyday life, not just on paper.
5. **Holistic view** – language, culture and practical circumstances belong together.

These values are descriptive, not claims of certification.

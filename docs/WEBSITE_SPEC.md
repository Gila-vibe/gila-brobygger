# Website specification

## Pages
- Homepage (`index.html`): short value proposition, overview of support areas, approach and contact CTA.
- Family consultant (`familiekonsulent.html`): clear service explanation, scope list, approach and contact panel.
- Interpretation and mentor services (from existing website content): `fremmedtolkning.html`, `telefontolkning.html`, `videotolkning.html`, `skriftligoversaettelse.html`, `mentorforloeb.html`.

## Navigation
- Forside
- Familiekonsulent
- Kontakt (email CTA)
- Service pages: Fremmedtolkning, Telefontolkning, Videotolkning, Skriftlig Oversættelse, Social Mentor

## Contact behavior
All contact CTAs use a direct `mailto:` link to `kontakt@gila-brobygger.dk`. Include a visible email address as a secondary contact route. A mailto link delegates to the visitor's email client; it is not a server-side form.

## Design direction
- Calm green palette, warm white surfaces and generous spacing.
- Clear headings and readable line lengths.
- Responsive layout with a collapsible mobile navigation.
- Avoid decorative motion that interferes with accessibility.

## Content rules
Only publish claims supplied or explicitly approved by the owner. Confirm exact service scope and professional qualifications before adding stronger claims such as “certified”, “licensed”, “specialist” or “guaranteed”.

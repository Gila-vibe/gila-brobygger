# Non-negotiable project invariants

1. The website language is Danish by default (`lang="da"`).
2. Contact email remains `kontakt@gila-brobygger.dk` unless the owner confirms a change.
3. No fabricated qualifications, registrations, testimonials, client outcomes, addresses, phone numbers, prices or availability.
4. No sensitive family or child information is collected by the static website.
5. No third-party analytics, advertising pixels, cookies, embedded chat or external form service without approval and privacy assessment.
6. Every navigation and contact link must have a meaningful destination.
7. Pages must remain usable at narrow mobile widths and with keyboard navigation.
8. Never deploy or overwrite production without owner approval and a restorable backup.
9. Keep a visible path to contact on the homepage and service page.
10. Do not describe an email link as a website form or claim that a message was delivered.
11. The family consulting service is one part of Gila Brobygger's offering; the website must not be treated as exclusively about family consulting.
